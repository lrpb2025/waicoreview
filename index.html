World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization ("WAICO") Review

What is WAICO?

WAICO is the first intergovernmental body dedicated to artificial intelligence ("AI"). Ten days after the first Global Dialogue on AI Governance (mandated by Resolution A/RES/79/325), 29 countries signed the founding agreement that established WAICO in Shanghai.

Why it matters

An urgent task facing countries worldwide is reaching an agreement on the safety and security of frontier AI technologies, not only because of cyber risks, but because of the potential for these tools to lower the barrier to creating bioweapons or chemical weapons, and the broader risk of humanity losing meaningful control over AI technological capacibilities. Unlike nuclear weapons, which were built and safeguarded by states with strong incentives not to use them, today's most powerful AI capabilities are largely developed by private companies driven by investor returns and profit which makes international coordination both harder and more urgent.

Purpose of the review

The review is grounded in three guiding aims:

  1. Strengthen inclusion and diversity
    Major AI governance frameworks are too often shaped without meaningful input from young people and communities historically excluded from international decision-making. This review works to broaden participation — reaching youth and underrepresented voices whose perspectives are essential to a governance framework that will affect them directly, but who have had limited opportunity to engage with the draft text.
  2. Reduce fragmentation across the ecosystem
    AI governance is being shaped simultaneously across multiple forums, frameworks, and initiatives, often with little coordination between them. By comparing WAICO against other existing and proposed texts, this review aims to surface overlaps, contradictions, and gaps — helping connect the many actors working on global AI governance and youth participation, rather than leaving them to work in silos.
  3. Shape a collective path forward
    A review is only useful if it leads somewhere. This process is designed to move beyond dialogue toward concrete outcomes: findings will inform side-event proposals, briefings, and interventions at the UN Global AI Governance Dialogues and other relevant UN forums — giving stakeholders a concrete path to turn their input into influence on the agreements that will govern AI globally.

Invitation to review

An intergovernmental agreement of this scale has the potential to shape how AI is governed worldwide for years to come. Ensuring it reflects broad, inclusive input is essential if it is to be further adopted.

The review of WAICO under www.waicoreview.com is an initiative of the Science-Policy Interface Platform (the "SPI Platform") of the UN Major Group for Children and Youth ("MGCY"). The SPI Platform is mandated to strengthen the evidence-base for policy through "the analysis, assessment, generation, dissemination and effective use of knowledge from both formal and informal sources" (Policies & Procedures, MGCY) and exists to bridge youth expertise in science, technology and innovation with the substantive, evidence-informed engagement that AI governance demands. This mandate is especially pressing in the AI governance space: the Co-Chairs' summary of the first Global Dialogue on AI Governance identified the underrepresentation of youth and children as a key outcome of that inaugural Dialogue. An independent, rigorously evidence-based review of WAICO conducted by SPI Platform directly addresses that gap ahead of the second Global Dialogue in May 2027.
