Data Protection Agreement

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. About this Data Protection Agreement

1.1 This Data Protection Agreement explains how personal data will be collected and used for the purpose of the review of the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization ("WAICO") as established under www.waicoreview.com.

1.2 Communication groups may be established to enable Participants to deliberate with one another for the purpose of the Review.

1.3 The Focal Point may collect and facilitate the exchange of views of Participants to generate the output of the Review.

1.4 The Focal Point agrees to process your personal data in the manner set out in this Data Protection Agreement.

1.5 You agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data as set out in this Data Protection Agreement.

1.6 By entering into this Data Protection Agreement, you undertake to comply with your obligations under this Data Protection Agreement, including those set out in sub-clauses 5.5 and 5.6.

2. Definitions

2.1 In this Data Protection Agreement, the following terms in the left-hand column shall have the meanings assigned to them in the corresponding right-hand column:

"Data Protection Laws" means applicable UK data protection laws, including the GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
"Digital Infrastructure" means third-party digital infrastructure used by the Focal Point for the operation of www.waicoreview.com, including web-hosting and deployment services, email services, operating systems, and productivity software.
"Focal Point" means the person coordinating the Review under www.waicoreview.com.
"GDPR" means the UK General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council).
"Participant" means any individual, corporate body, partnership, organisation, association, or other person whether incorporated or unincorporated, who has signed up as a participant in the Review, and "Participants" shall be construed similarly.
"Review" means the review of WAICO under www.waicoreview.com.
"UK" means the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.
"you" means you, whether in your personal capacity as an individual, or as a corporate body, partnership, organisation, association, or other person whether incorporated or unincorporated, who has signed up to become a Participant in the Review, and "your" shall be construed similarly.

3. What data will be collected?

3.1 Only data reasonably necessary for the purpose of:

(a) generating the substantive output of the Review;

(b) facilitating smooth communication between Participants; and

(c) verifying the credibility of the Review;

will be collected.

3.2 In relation to sub-clause 3.1, such data may include:

(a) your name;

(b) your email address;

(c) your telephone/mobile number;

(d) your organisation, affiliation or role;

(e) your region; and

(e) information revealing your political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs.

3.3 Please do not provide personal data unless it is specifically requested and necessary.

4. Purposes for which your data may be used

4.1 Your data will be used solely for purposes connected with the Review, including:

(a) organising and administering communication groups created for the purpose of the Review;

(b) enabling Participants in the Review to communicate with one another;

(c) facilitating discussion and deliberation for the purpose of the Review;

(d) contacting you about matters directly related to the Review;

(e) creating a record for verifying the credibility of the Review; and

(f) in relation to information revealing your political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, to form the input that will be processed to generate the output of the Review.

4.2 Your personal data will not be used for unrelated marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Who will see your data

5.1 Important: If you participate in the Review, the name, contact details, and any other information that you provide may be made visible to other Participants.

5.2 The sharing of the name and contact details referred to in sub-clause 5.1 is necessary to enable Participants to communicate with one another in connection with the Review.

5.3 Important: If you participate in the Review, information revealing your political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs may be made visible to other Participants.

5.4 The sharing of information revealing your political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs is necessary to facilitate the exchange of Participant views and opinions to generate the output of the Review.

5.5 By agreeing to this Data Protection Agreement you are cofirming your consent to the sharing of your data in the manner referred to in sub-clauses 5.1 and 5.2.

5.6 Data you provide will be accessible to the Focal Point and may also become accessible to the providers of any Digital Infrastructure used by the Focal Point to collect, store, and manage personal data and other information for the purposes of the Review.

5.7 You are responsible for the handling of any personal data disclosed to you through the Review, including any onward use, disclosure, storage or deletion of that data, and for complying with Data Protection Laws in respect of that handling.

5.8 You must treat the personal data of other Participants in accordance with applicable Data Protection Laws.

6. Exclusions of liability

6.1 The Focal Point accepts no liability for any loss, damage, infringement of Data Protection Laws or other harms arising from the acts or omissions of you or any other Participants in relation to personal data and any other information disclosed through the Review.

6.2 To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Focal Point accepts no liability for any loss, damage, or harm arising from any fault, failure, outage, disruption, or security incident affecting the Digital Infrastructure, where this is outside the Focal Point's reasonable control.

7. Your choice and legal basis

7.1 The Focal Point will process your personal data only where there is an appropriate legal basis under Data Protection Laws.

7.2 If you choose to provide data revealing your political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs you will be asked to give your explicit consent to its processing as described under this Data Protection Agreement for the purpose of the Review.

7.3 You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the data protection officer at dataprotection@waicoreview.com.

7.4 Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place:

(a) before consent was withdrawn; or

(b) before the data protection officer could reasonably have been expected to acknowledge receipt of a sub-clause 7.3 notice of withdrawal.

8. How long will your data be kept?

8.1 This clause 8 is without prejudice to clause 9.

8.2 Your personal data will be retained for as long as is necessary for undertaking the Review.

8.3 Unless there is a legitimate reason to retain your personal data for longer, personal data you have provided will be deleted or anonymised in the following timeframes:

(a) in the event that you withdraw or otherwise cease to participate in the Review, within eight weeks of the date on which you notify the Focal Point of such withdrawal or cessation to fp@waicoreview.com;

(b) in the event of a discontinuation of the Review, within twelve weeks of the date on which the Focal Point confirms such discontinuation via email; or

(c) in the event of completion of the Review, within twelve weeks of the date on which the Focal Point confirms such completion via email.

8.4 For the purposes of sub-clauses 8.2(b) and 8.2(c), the Focal Point will send confirmation emails from fp@waicoreview.com.

8.5 Personal data may be retained for longer where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

9. Your data protection rights

9.1 In accordance with the Data Protection Laws, you may have the right to:

(a) request access to your personal data;

(b) request correction of inaccurate personal data;

(c) request deletion of your personal data;

(d) request restriction of processing of your personal data;

(e) object to certain processing of your personal data;

(f) request transfer of your personal data where applicable; and

(g) withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

9.2 To exercise your rights or ask questions about this Data Protection Agreement, please contact dataprotection@waicoreview.com.

10. Complaints

10.1 If you are concerned about how your personal data has been handled or will be handled, please make contact per sub-clause 9.2 and set out your concern.

10.2 If you remain dissatisfied, you may have the right to complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority. For processing carried out in the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Severability

11.1 If any provision of this Data Protection Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be removed only to the extent necessary to remove such invalidity or unenforceability, and the remaining provisions of the Data Protection Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.

12. Changes to this Data Protection Agreement

12.1 The Focal Point may amend this Data Protection Agreement from time to time, including to reflect changes to the operation of the Review, the way personal data is processed, or applicable legal requirements, and you agree to be bound by any such amendment provided the amendment is reasonable.

12.2 The data protection officer will notify you of any material amendment made to the Data Protection Agreement by the email provided by you or other reasonable means of communication.

12.3 Any amendment will take effect on the date stated in the relevant notice. Your continued participation in the Review after that date constitutes your acceptance of the amended Data Protection Agreement. If you do not agree to an amendment, you must cease participation in the Review and may request deletion of your personal data in accordance with this Data Protection Agreement and Data Protection Laws.

13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

13.1 This Data Protection Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisidction to adjudicate on any dispute arising in relation to this Data Protection Agreement.
