Data Protection Agreement

Last updated: 22 September 2026

1. About this Data Protection Agreement

1.1 This Data Protection Agreement explains how personal data and other information will be collected and used for the purpose of the review of the World International Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization ("WAICO") as established under www.waicoreview.com.

1.2 Communication groups may be established to enable Participants to deliberate with one another for the purpose of the Review.

1.3 The Coordinator agrees to process your personal data in the manner set out in this Data Protection Agreement.

1.4 You agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal data as set out in this Data Protection Agreement.

1.5 By entering into this Data Protection Agreement, you undertake to comply with your obligations, including those set out at sub-clauses 5.5 and 5.6.

2. Definitions

2.1 In this Data Protection Agreement, the following terms in the lefthand column shall have the meanings assigned to them in the corresponding righthand column:

"Coordinator" means the individual coordinating the Review under www.waicoreview.com, and includes any successor(s) appointed to that role.
"Data Protection Laws" means applicable UK data-protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament of the Council) and the Data Protection Act 2018.
"Digital Infrastructure" means third-party digital infrastructure used by the Coordinator for the operation of www.waicoreview.com, including web-hosting and deployment services, email services, operating systems, and productivity software.
"Participant" means any individual, corporate body, partnership, organisation, association, or other person whether incorporated or unincorporated, who has signed up as a participant in the Review, and "Participants" shall be construed similarly.
"Review" means the review of the WAICO under www.waicoreview.com.
"UK" means the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.
"you" means you, whether in your personal capacity as an individual, or as a corporate body, partnership, organisation, association, or other person whether incorporated or unincorporated, who has signed up to become a Participant in the Review, and "your" shall be construed similarly.

3. What data will be collected?

3.1 Only data reasonably necessary for the purpose of facilitating smooth communication between Participants and verifying the credentials of the Review will be collected. This may include:

(a) your name;

(b) your email address;

(c) your telephone/mobile number, where necessary or voluntarily provided; and

(d) your organisation, affiliation or role, where relevant to the Review.

3.2 Please do not provide personal data unless it is specifically requested and necessary.

4. Purposes for which your data may be used

4.1 Your data will be used solely for purposes connected with the Review, including:

(a) organising and administering the Review group;

(b) enabling participants in the Review to communicate with one another;

(c) facilitating discussion and deliberation for the purpose of the Review;

(d) contacting you about matters directly related to the Review; and

(e) creating a record for the purpose of verifying the credentials of the Review.

4.2 Your personal data will not be used for unrelated marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Who will see your data

5.1 Important: If you participate in the Review, the name, contact details, and any other information that you provide may be made visible to other Participants.

5.2 The sharing of the name and contact details referred to in clause 5.1 is necessary to enable Participants to communicate with one another in connection with the Review.

5.3 Before your contact details are shared with other Participants in the manner set out in clause 5.2, you will be given the opportunity to agree to this disclosure.

5.4 Data you provide will be accessible to the Coordinator and may also become accessible to the providers of any Digital Infrastructure used by the Coordinator to collect, store, and manage personal data and other information for the purposes of the Review.

5.5 You are responsible for the handling of any personal data disclosed to you through the Review, including any onward use, disclosure, storage or deletion of that data, and for complying with Data Protection Laws in respect of that handling.

5.6 You must treat the personal data of other Participants in accordance with applicable Data Protection Laws.

6. Exclusions of liability

6.1 The Coordinator accepts no liability for any loss, damage, infringement of Data Protection Laws or other harms arising from the acts or omissions of you or any other Participants in relation to personal data and any other information disclosed through the Review.

6.2 To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Coordinator accepts no liability for any loss, damage, or harm arising from any fault, failure, outage, disruption, or security incident affecting the Digital Infrastructure, where this is outside the Coordinator's reasonable control.

6.3 Nothing in this clause 6 excludes or limits the Coordinator's liability to the extent that liability cannot be lawfully excluded or limited, including liability under Data Protection Laws.

7. Your choice and legal basis

7.1 The Coordinator will process your personal data only where there is an appropriate legal basis under Data Protection Laws.

7.2 Where your contact details are to be made visible to other Participants, you will be asked to provide a clear affirmative indication that you agree to this disclosure.

7.3 You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the Coordinator at dataprotection@waicoreview.com.

7.4 Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place:

(a) before consent was withdrawn; or

(b) before the Coordinator could reasonably have been expected to have acknowledged receipt of a clause 7.3 notice of withdrawal.

7.5 You are not required to provide information that is not necessary for participation in the Review.

8. How long will your data be kept?

8.1 Your personal data will be retained for as long as is necessary for undertaking the Review.

8.2 Unless there is a legitimate reason to retain your personal data for longer, Participant contact information will be deleted or anonymised within 90 calendar days following completion of the Review.

8.3 Information may be retained for longer where necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

9. Your data-protection rights

9.1 In accordance with the Data Protection Laws, you may have the right to:

(a) request access to your personal data;

(b) request correction of inaccurate personal data;

(c) request deletion of your personal data;

(d) request restriction of processing of your personal data;

(e) object to certain processing of your personal data;

(f) request transfer of your personal data where applicable; and

(g) withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

9.2 To exercise your rights or ask questions about this Data Protection Agreement, please contact dataprotection@waicoreview.com.

10. Complaints

10.1 If you are concerned about how your personal data has been handled or will be handled, please make contact per clause 9.2 and set out your concern.

10.2 If you remain dissatisfied, you may have the right to complain to the relevant data-protection supervisory authority. For processing carried out in the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Changes to this Data Protection Agreement

11.1 The Coordinator may amend this Data Protection Agreement from time to time, including to reflect changes to the Review, the way personal data is processed, or applicable legal requirements, and you agree to be bound by any such amendment.

11.2 The Coordinator will notify you of any material amendment by the email provided by you or other reasonable means of communication.

11.3 Any amendment will take effect on the date stated in the relevant notice. Your continued participation in the Review after that date constitutes your acceptance of the amended Data Protection Agreement. If you do not agree to an amendment, you must cease participation in the Review and may request deletion of your personal data in accordance with this Data Protection Agreement and Data Protection Laws.
