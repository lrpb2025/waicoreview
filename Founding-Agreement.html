Where can I find the official WAICO agreement text?

You cannot read the official text of the WAICO founding agreement anywhere online: Whilst several signatory governments have issued official statements or summaries confirming membership or anticipated membership, no government has published the full text of the WAICO founding agreement.

Below is a non-exhaustive list of signatory governments that have published official statements or summaries about the WAICO agreement on their own government websites. Click on a country to visit that page:
  • China — Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China
  • Lesotho — Government of Lesotho, official website
  • South Africa — South African Government News Agency (SAnews.gov.za)
  • Pakistan — Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Pakistan

Where can I find the WAICO agreement text?

Digital Watch Observatory (dig.watch) has published the text of the WAICO founding agreement. The platform is operated by DiploFoundation, a non-profit foundation established by the governments of Malta and Switzerland, in partnership with the Geneva Internet Platform and the Internet Society. It is widely used by diplomats and policy practitioners as a neutral, independent reference for internet governance and digital policy developments. It currently represents the most complete and reliable publicly accessible version of the WAICO agreement text.
